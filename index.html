I'm a Master's student in Computer Engineering and Systems at the State University of Maranhão (UEMA), with an exchange period at Duale Hochschule Sachsen (DHSN) in Riesa, Germany. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the Federal University of Western Pará (UFOPA).

My research areas are artificial intelligence and data science applied to the legal domain, using text mining, natural language processing and large language models to extract knowledge from textual data. I also work on scientometrics and social network analysis.

Current Work

  • Assisted generation of judicial drafts with LLMs — my master's thesis, within the UEMA–TJMA technical cooperation
  • Scientometrics — collaboration networks of CNPq productivity fellows and predatory publishing among Brazilian researchers

Recent Publications
