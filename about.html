Fernando works where computing meets people and society, using AI, text mining, NLP and data science to study how knowledge is produced and shared. He began his path at UFOPA, a young public university in the heart of the Brazilian Amazon, which he joined as an Indigenous student. He is now a Master's scholarship holder at UEMA, applying AI to the Brazilian justice system with TJMA, and has spent an exchange period at Duale Hochschule Sachsen in Germany.
